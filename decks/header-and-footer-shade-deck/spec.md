## Lede

Four short cards on a slight shade difference between the header and footer bands and the white Home page. The live header already uses Warm White #FFFCF7, the brand-color logo, Share at the top left, and the menu at the top right. These cards keep that header and the Built by Machan footer (no link), and only change the band shade, or add a hairline at the join. Each pair shows the hex values. Pick one per card, or tap Machan Decides, and use Notes for anything to tweak.

## Cards

### 1. Header Shade

Which slight shade should the header band use against the white page?

- **A. Deeper Warm Cream**: A slightly deeper warm cream header, #F5EDDF, against the white page #FFFFFF. Share stays top left, the logo stays centered, and the menu stays top right.
- **B. Soft Sand Tint**: A soft sand tint header, #EFE3CE, against the same white page #FFFFFF. The logo, Share, and menu stay as they are on the live site.

Both options stay light and only a little different from the page #FFFFFF. Green is kept off the header band. These are not the Header Color Deck tokens (Warm White #FFFCF7, Cream #F6F1E7, Sand #EDE6D9).

### 2. Footer Shade

Which slight shade should the footer band use when Built by Machan is pulled up?

- **A. Deeper Warm Cream**: A slightly deeper warm cream footer, #F5EDDF, behind Built by Machan, against the white page #FFFFFF. The line has no link.
- **B. Soft Sand Tint**: A soft sand tint footer, #EFE3CE, behind Built by Machan, against the same white page #FFFFFF. The line has no link.

Shown as the footer looks when you pull up at the bottom of Home. Both options stay light and only a little different from the page #FFFFFF.

### 3. Match Or Differ

Should the header and footer use the same shade, or two slightly different ones?

- **A. Same Shade**: Header and footer both use Deeper Warm Cream #F5EDDF against the white page #FFFFFF.
- **B. Different Shades**: The header uses Deeper Warm Cream #F5EDDF, and the footer uses Soft Sand Tint #EFE3CE, against the same white page #FFFFFF.

Each option shows the top of Home and the pulled-up footer together, so you can judge the two bands side by side.

### 4. Hairline At The Join

Should a thin hairline sit at the join between the bands and the page?

- **A. No Hairline**: The bands use Deeper Warm Cream #F5EDDF against the page #FFFFFF, with no hairline. The shade alone marks the join.
- **B. Hairline**: The same #F5EDDF bands, with a thin hairline in #E6E1D8 at the join with the page, on the header card and on the footer band.

This is the join at rest, not the scrolled header. The Warm Shadow pick from the Header Color Deck still applies once you scroll.
