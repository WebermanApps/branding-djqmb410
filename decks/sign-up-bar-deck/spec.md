## Lede

Three cards on how the sign-up bottom bar should be laid out. Develop is holding the current sign-up update until you pick. Each option is a real render of How Big Is Your Home?, with the house band, at 360, 390 and 430 wide. The first card is two rows versus one row. The second is which buttons sit in a one-row bar. The third is a tighter one-row idea, with Back as an icon and Start Over as a link above the bar. Pick one per card, or tap Machan Decides, and use Notes for anything to tweak.

## Cards

### 1. Bar Layout

Which bar layout should sign-up use?

- **A. Tabs Over Next**: Develop’s version: small Back, Home and Start Over tabs over a full-width Next, matching the Home bar.
- **B. One Row, Back, Start Over And Next**: One row in the cream Home bar style, with Next filled and Back and Start Over outlined, all the same height.

Both options use the cream Home bar color #F5EDDF, with the hairline and sheen. A, Tabs Over Next, is Develop’s version as it is in the current update: the bar is 111px tall. Air under the Stories card is 22px at 360 wide, 6px at 390 wide, and 98px at 430 wide. The 6px at 390 wide is under the 8px target, because the tighter step spacing only applies at 360×640. B, One Row, Back, Start Over And Next, is 61px tall. Air is 72px at 360 wide, 56px at 390 wide, and 148px at 430 wide. Back and Start Over are outlined, Next is filled, and all three are 44px tall. Labels do not wrap or clip at 360.

### 2. One-Row Buttons

If the bar is one row, which buttons should sit in it?

- **A. One Row, Back, Start Over And Next**: Back, Start Over and Next in one row, with Next filled and the other two outlined.
- **B. One Row, Back, Home And Next**: Back, Home and Next in one row, in the same cream style, with Next filled and the other two outlined.

Both options are one row, 61px tall, in the same cream Home bar style, with Next filled and the others outlined, all 44px tall. Air is 72px at 360 wide, 56px at 390 wide, and 148px at 430 wide. The only change is the middle button: Start Over, or Home.

### 3. Start Over Placement

Should Start Over stay in the row, or sit above the bar as a link?

- **A. One Row, Back, Start Over And Next**: Start Over stays in the row as an outlined button, beside Back, with a filled Next.
- **B. Icon Back, Wide Next, And A Start Over Link**: Back becomes a 44px icon button, Next goes wide, and Start Over moves up to a text link just above the cream bar.

A keeps the one-row bar from the earlier cards: Back, Start Over And Next, 61px tall, with 72px, 56px and 148px of air at 360, 390 and 430 wide. B moves Start Over up to a 44px text link just above the cream bar. The bar itself is 61px, with an icon Back and a wide Next. Air under the Stories card, down to that link, is 28px at 360 wide, 12px at 390 wide, and 104px at 430 wide. The Back icon keeps a 44px tap, and the word Back stays in the accessible name.
