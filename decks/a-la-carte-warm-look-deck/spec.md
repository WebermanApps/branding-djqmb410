# À La Carte Warm Look Deck

Style Swipe · Smart Upkeep · Made Nov 13, 2026

Six cards on a warmer look for Tailor Your Plan, the Home section that was Shop À La Carte. The grounds are cream and white, with terracotta and charcoal accents, and no green as the main look. There is no carousel. Every frame uses the title Tailor Your Plan and the line Members can tailor their plan or add one-time services. Pick one per card, or tap Machan Decides, and use Notes for anything to tweak.
