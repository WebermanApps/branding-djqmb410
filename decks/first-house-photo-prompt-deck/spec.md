# First House Photo Prompt Deck

Style Swipe · Smart Upkeep · Made Nov 12, 2026

Five questions about a new, optional photo of the front of the house. This step is not in the funnel today — the pictures are overlays on the real post-sign-up chrome at 390px. The heading, why-line, guidance, privacy line, actions, chips, thumbs, nudges, and short skip line are Legal’s approved wording. Pick one per card, or tap Machan Decides, and use Notes for anything to tweak.

## Request
New-feature deck: there is no photo step in the funnel today, and the You're In Your Home card is off. When to ask, prompt layout, camera overlay, after a weak shot, and if they skip. Legal heading “Add A Photo Of Your Home (Optional)” and “Photos are handled as described in our Privacy Policy.” Short skip line only. No Street View, no long skip, no member-app later path. Use fp-a, fp-b, and cropped fp-d from Video & Photo. Drawn thumbs only.

- B · After Sign-Up, On You're In: They finish the 10 steps first. A new Your Home card on You're In offers the same three actions, with Refer A Neighbor under it.
