## Ask

Machan, Nov 14 2026, 3:10 PM ICT, relayed by Grok: “i don't want to use a real image of a house”, “it should be graphics only for share”, “redo deck please”. Remake the Home Share Image Deck as a graphics-only deck for the link preview image shown when smartupkeep.ai is texted or posted. Use no real photos, only brand graphics: the logo, a line-art or illustrated house (vector, not traced from a photo), and $139 where a price is shown (Essentials, the approved price). Light cream or warm backgrounds, never a green header band, no gold, no dark colors.

## Lede

Four cards on a graphics-only 1200×630 share image for when someone texts or posts smartupkeep.ai. No real house photos. Each option shows the full share image, then a preview of how it looks in an iMessage card and an X link card. The locked words stay: Preventive Home Maintenance, and Starting At $139/mo when a price is shown. Pick one per card, or tap Machan Decides, and use Notes for anything to tweak.

## Cards

### 1. House Style

Should the share image use a line-art house or an illustrated house?

- **A. Line-Art House**: A simple line-art house in ink, with a terracotta roof line, next to the logo and Starting At $139/mo, on cream #FAF6EE.
- **B. Illustrated House**: A filled illustrated house with a terracotta roof and cream walls, next to the same logo and Starting At $139/mo, on cream #FAF6EE.

Both houses are original vectors, not traced from a photo. The locked words and the $139 Essentials price stay the same.

### 2. Price On The Image

Should the share image show Starting At $139/mo?

- **A. With $139**: The share image shows Preventive Home Maintenance and Starting At $139/mo, the approved Essentials price.
- **B. Without A Price**: The share image shows Preventive Home Maintenance only, with no price on the picture. The iMessage and X captions also drop the price line.

Both options use the illustrated house on cream. The live share line can still mention the price even if it is not on the picture.

### 3. Layout

Should the logo and words lead, or should the house lead?

- **A. Logo-Led**: The logo and words sit on the left, with a house of modest size on the right.
- **B. House-Led**: A larger house fills the right side, and the logo and words sit in a tighter stack on the left.

Both options use the illustrated house, Starting At $139/mo, and cream. Only the layout changes.

### 4. Background

Which light background should the share image use?

- **A. Cream**: Cream #FAF6EE, the familiar warm paper.
- **B. Warm Sand**: Warm Sand #F1E6D4, a slightly deeper warm tint.

Both options use the illustrated house, the logo-led layout, and Starting At $139/mo. Only the background changes.
