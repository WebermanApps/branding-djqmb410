Build a Share Image Polish Deck from his locked picks (Logo-Led, Illustrated House, With $139, Cream #FAF6EE). ## Lede

Five short cards on a tighter 1200×630 share image for when someone texts or posts smartupkeep.ai. Every option keeps the locked picks: a logo-led layout, the illustrated house, Starting At $139/mo, and cream #FAF6EE. No real house photos. Each option shows the full share image, then a preview of how it looks in an iMessage card and an X link card. Pick one per card, or tap Machan Decides, and use Notes for anything to tweak.

## Cards

### 1. Scale

Should the share image stay at the current size, or fill the frame?

- **A. Current Size**: The logo, words, and house stay at today’s size, with more cream around them.
- **B. Filling The Frame**: The logo, words, and house grow and sit with tight margins inside the center square used by some link previews. The sides of the wide image stay cream on purpose, so a square crop still shows everything.

Both options keep the illustrated house, Starting At $139/mo, and cream #FAF6EE. Only the size and how tightly the picture is filled change.

### 2. Logo

Should a small logo sit above the headline, or should a large logo be the headline?

- **A. Small Logo Above The Headline**: The logo sits above Preventive Home Maintenance, with Starting At $139/mo under the words.
- **B. Large Logo As The Headline**: A large logo leads the picture, with Preventive Home Maintenance as a short line under it, then Starting At $139/mo.

Both options use the larger, filled layout, the illustrated house, and cream #FAF6EE. Only how the logo and the headline are arranged changes.

### 3. House Details

Should the house stay plain, or add small brand details?

- **A. Plain House**: The illustrated house with a terracotta roof and cream walls, and no extra details.
- **B. House With Brand Details**: The same illustrated house, with a terracotta stoop and window boxes, a short ground line, a small tool, and a checkmark badge.

Both options stay illustrated. No real photos are used. The small green shrubs stay, and green is not the main color. These are shown at the larger, filled size.

### 4. Price

Should Starting At $139/mo stay as plain text, or sit in a terracotta pill?

- **A. Plain Text Price**: Starting At $139/mo is set in terracotta type, with no shape behind it.
- **B. Terracotta Price Pill**: Starting At $139/mo sits in a rounded terracotta pill, with cream type.

The words stay Starting At $139/mo, the approved Essentials price. Both options use the filled layout, the illustrated house, and cream #FAF6EE. Only the price treatment changes.

### 5. Background

Should the background stay flat cream, or add a soft warm sand band behind the house?

- **A. Flat Cream**: The whole picture sits on cream #FAF6EE, with no extra band.
- **B. Warm Sand Band**: The picture stays on cream #FAF6EE, with a soft rounded warm sand band, #E8D5B8, behind the house.

This is a band behind the house, not a header or footer band. Both options use the filled layout, the illustrated house, and Starting At $139/mo. Only the band changes.
