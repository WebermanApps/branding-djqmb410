## Cards

### Your Details Screen
Name, phone and email sit inside the long Accept page today. Keep that, or give them their own step?
- A · Inside Accept: Today: under the recap. At 390 only 2 of 3 fields are above the fold, and Accept is about 2,350px tall.
- B · Its Own Step: Your Details on its own screen before Accept: all 3 fields and Next above the fold.

### Name First
Should name, phone and email be asked together or name first?
- A · All Three Together: Today: Full Name, Phone and Email in one card.
- B · Name First, Then Phone And Email: Full Name alone on a short screen, then Phone and Email on the next.

### Phone Or Email First
Which comes first after the name?
- A · Phone, Then Email: Today: Full Name, Phone, Email.
- B · Email, Then Phone: Full Name, Email, Phone. Email is their member app login.

### Service Address Line
Where should the Service Address with Edit sit in Your Details?
- A · Small Line At The End: Today: a small line under Email.
- B · Highlighted At The Top: A soft lagoon row at the top of the card, so they check the address first.

### Have A Code?
How should the Refer A Neighbor code field show?
- A · Hidden Behind A Link: Today: a Have A Code? link opens the field.

### Signature Start
Which signature mode should be picked first?
- A · Draw First: Today: Draw is picked, with an empty pad.

### Price Note

### Field Height
How tall should the text fields be?
